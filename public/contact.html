GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

We're happy to help with any other questions over WhatsApp.

How do I make a booking?

Choose your room, dates and number of guests, then submit a booking request. The team will confirm availability directly.

Can I pay online?

There is no payment collection on this website. Petra Lodge will confirm your booking and explain payment arrangements directly.

Is Wi-Fi included?

Yes, unlimited Wi-Fi is listed as a feature of the Single Suite, Double Room and Family Room.

How many guests can each room accommodate?

Single Suite: 1 guest. Double Room: 2 guests. Family Room: 3 guests. A room with three separate single beds can be enquired about directly; ask for its current rate.

Do you offer spa or conference facilities?

The previous Petra Lodge website described spa and conference services. Please enquire directly for current availability and details.
